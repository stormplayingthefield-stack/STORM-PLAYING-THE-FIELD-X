### 2. Infrared / Thermal Signature Management
- **Active Thermal Cloaking:** Adaptive electrochromic panels + micro-fluidic cooling channels that redistribute heat across the hull or vent it directionally.
- **Plasma Drive Exhaust Management:** Dual-mode exhaust — high-efficiency "cold" plasma mode for stealth operations significantly reduces IR plume.
- **Night Mode Configuration:** Hull coated in ultra-low-emissivity metamaterials. Heat signature approaches cosmic background radiation levels in deep space.

### 3. Electromagnetic Emission Control (EMCON)
- **Low-Probability-of-Intercept (LPI) Communications:** Tight-beam laser and quantum-encrypted 6G/IoT mesh networking.
- **Emission-Absent Mode:** Full passive sensor operation using distributed aperture systems and AI-driven sensor fusion.
- **Magnetic Anomaly Reduction:** Demagnetized composite hull + active magnetic field cancellation coils.

### 4. Visual & Optical Stealth
- **Adaptive Camouflage Skin:** Metasurface panels capable of real-time color, texture, and luminosity matching with background (nebulae, planetary surfaces, or starfields).
- **Light-Bending Edge Tech:** Micro-prismatic arrays along leading edges for partial optical cloaking.
- **Night Mode Visual Profile:** Matte black non-reflective coating with minimal running lights — only faint cyan plasma glows when systems are active.

### 5. Acoustic & Vibration Suppression
- **Active Noise Cancellation:** Piezoelectric actuators throughout the airframe.
- **Ducted Fan Silencing:** Variable-geometry intake baffles and resonant absorbers optimized for eVTOL hover and transition phases.
